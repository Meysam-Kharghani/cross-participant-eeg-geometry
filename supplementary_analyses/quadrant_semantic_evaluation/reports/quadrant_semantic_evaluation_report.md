**Status:** separate post-hoc branch; prespecified primary analysis not modified.

## prespecified hypotheses

- H1 PRIMARY: Valence × Arousal
- H2 SECONDARY: Liking × Arousal
- H3 SECONDARY: Valence × Liking

High/low is person-relative within each split (sign of within-participant z score).
Discovery alone determines the four-family ↔ four-quadrant bijection; the same mapping is transferred unchanged to Validation and Confirmation.

## Held-out replication gates

- H1 valence_x_arousal: NOT SUPPORTED (Validation=False, Confirmation=False)
- H2 liking_x_arousal: NOT SUPPORTED (Validation=False, Confirmation=True)
- H3 valence_x_liking: NOT SUPPORTED (Validation=False, Confirmation=False)

## Interpretation boundary

A PASS would support only an internally replicated statistical alignment between K4 assignments and that pre-specified person-relative report quadrant. It would not identify true emotions, causal generators, biological independence, or external validity.

A failure means that specific quadrant interpretation is not internally replicated under this prespecified test.
